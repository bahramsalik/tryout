Five whys: why can’t new grads get hired?

WHY 1

Companies prefer experienced people over recent grads

WHY 2

They avoid the risk of hiring untrained, unproven people

WHY 3

So “entry-level” postings ask for 1–3+ years of experience

WHY 4

Meanwhile, classrooms teach theory more than job skills

ROOT

A gap between what’s taught and what the job needs, and no trusted way to prove practical skill without already having had a job.
