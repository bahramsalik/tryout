## Checked but not used (dropped or reworded)

- **"Internships cut underemployment by ~49%"**: this appears in secondary coverage of *Talent Disrupted* (Poets&Quants), but I couldn't find the figure in the report's text, so it's **dropped**.
- **SHRM executive cost per hire ($28,329)**: seen only on secondary sites, and SHRM's 2025 report implies a different 2022 base. It isn't needed, so it's **not used**.
- **An exact NACE percentage** for "employers prefer candidates with internship experience": the Job Outlook 2026 article gives a ranking, not a percentage, so we state the ranking only.
- **"A bad hire costs 30% of first-year salary"**: this is widely attributed to the US Department of Labor, but I found no primary source. **Not used.**
- **"Half of postings titled entry-level require ~3 years"** (Money.com, a narrower TalentWorks sample of 17,400 titles): this is consistent with #1 but redundant. **Not used.**
